BNS base station: wiring

One 5 V wall brick powers both panels, the XMC4700 Relax Kit and the receiver XIAO. Diagrams are schematic, not to scale.

1. Overview

+5 V GND UART: XIAO TX → kit RX UART: kit TX → XIAO RX HUB75 data (16 wires)  diode, bar = band end
Never connect anything to a VDD3.3 / 3V3 pin, and don't run +5 V into the kit or XIAO without its diode.
Relax Kit: safe to plug the Mac into X101 any time. The kit already has Schottky diodes (D101/D103, BAS3010A) between each USB jack and VDD5, so nothing flows back into the Mac; SS54 stops the Mac from powering the panels.
XIAO: different. Its 5V pin is wired straight to the USB-C VBUS (schematic sheet 3: header pin 14 = VBUS; the on-board Schottky D1 sits after that, before the 3.3 V buck). With the 5V wire attached and the USB-C plugged into the Mac, the brick and the Mac's port are tied together through SS14. Pull the XIAO's 5V jumper before plugging it into the Mac (GND, TX, RX can stay).

2. What a "bus" is

A bus is one shared connection point. Every wire you land on the red one carries +5 V; every wire on the black one is ground. The easiest version is a Wago 221-415 lever connector: flip a lever up, push in a stripped wire, flip it down. You need exactly 5 slots on each, so two connectors cover everything.

The diodes go in-line: solder one into the wire, then slide heat-shrink over it. The silver/white band points toward the board being powered.

3. Pin maps

HUB75 connector on Panel 1 "IN", looking at the pins

Not in our datasheets: the reference folder only has the FM6124 driver chip datasheet, not the panel's connector. This is the standard HUB75 (64×64, E on pin 8) pinout. Pin 1 is marked with a triangle or "1" on the panel silkscreen. If your panel's silkscreen labels differ from this drawing, trust the silkscreen.

Relax Kit headers X1 / X2, top view (same orientation as Figure 7 in the kit manual)

Pin numbers are also printed on the bottom of the PCB; check against that before plugging in.

Wire colours assume the standard rainbow ribbon: pin 1 (the edge with the ▶ / stripe) is brown, then red, orange, yellow, green, blue, violet, grey, white, black, and it repeats (pin 11 brown … pin 16 blue). If your pin-1 wire is a different colour, the colours here are off but the pin numbers are still right: go by the pin number. Both HUB75 grounds (pins 4 and 16) must be connected.

Logic level, works but out of spec: the FM6124 datasheet gives VIH = 0.7·VDD = 3.5 V at 5 V, and the XMC4700 drives 3.3 V. Most panels tolerate this (many buffer inputs with a 74HC245). If you see flicker or noise, add a 74HCT245 level shifter in the ribbon.

5. Bring-up order

  1. Brick + jack + fuse + buses only. Plug in, meter the buses: 5.0–5.2 V, red bus positive.
  2. Add kit and XIAO power (no panels). Green power LED on the kit lights; VDD5 reads about 4.6–4.8 V (diode drop is normal).
  3. Add panel power with caps, then the HUB75 ribbon, then run the display.
  4. Add the UART wires. Plug the Mac into X101 whenever you like. For the XIAO, pull its 5V jumper first, then plug in USB-C.
Software: base/src/input.c reads the receiver on U1C0 (P2.15/P2.14), and the firmware drives the 128-wide chain (HUB75_PANELS=2 in base/Makefile); panel 0 = Panel 1, wired to the XMC.
